Our Story

A Dream Brewed
in Los Santos

Blossom Breweries was founded by Sakura Akiyama, a Japanese brewmaster who brought her craft to Los Santos with two suitcases, a notebook full of recipes, and an unshakeable belief that great beer and Japanese brewing tradition belonged in the same glass.

Sakura spent years in Japan studying the relationship between traditional brewing craft and modern flavour, convinced that the two worlds had more in common than most people realised. Blossom is the result of that conviction: a brewery where a cold Meadow Lager sits comfortably next to a ceremonial Sakura Sake, and where every pour tells a story about craft, patience, and culture.

Founded in 2025, Blossom Breweries operates out of Los Santos, San Andreas. Sakura runs every aspect of the brewery with the same philosophy she carried across the Pacific: if it isn't worth being proud of, it doesn't leave the tank.

How We Brew

The old way.
The only way.

01

Select

We choose only the finest local barley, hand-selected hops from farms we've known for decades, and whole-flower botanicals sourced from the hillsides above Los Santos.

02

Mash

Slow-mashed in our original copper vessels over several hours, the grain releases its full depth. Patience, not pressure, is our method. It always has been.

03

Ferment

Open fermentation in our stone-floored conditioning room, using the same house yeast strain since 1991. It knows these walls. It knows this beer. We trust it completely.

04

Condition

Cold-conditioned for a minimum of three weeks, each batch rests until the beer itself signals readiness. Not until a deadline has passed, but until it is truly finished.
